# dima design brief

## Product

dima is a trusted conversational analytics product. It turns business-language
questions into modeled, guarded, inspectable analytical results.

## Primary audience

Decision-making teams who need fast answers and data teams who need definitions,
permissions, SQL, and provenance to remain visible.

## Ranked principles

1. Evidence before claims: show the mechanism, not invented proof.
2. Product before decoration: every visual explains context, validation, or reuse.
3. Editorial clarity: strong hierarchy, asymmetric rhythm, restrained surfaces.
4. Trust remains visible: provenance, state, and capability status are never hidden.
5. Motion explains relationships and state; it never delays interaction.

## Design intent

- CRAFT_LEVEL: 9
- MOTION_INTENSITY: 6
- VISUAL_DENSITY: 6
- DESIGN_VARIANCE: 7
- Signature detail: a restrained evidence thread connecting context to outcome.
- Reference: UpcyMan's image density and motion choreography, never its brand,
  copy, assets, colors, or industry positioning.

## Success

A first-time visitor can explain what dima does, how an answer is constrained, and
how to request a demo after scanning the home page. Marketing pages remain usable
in light/dark themes, on touch devices, by keyboard, and with reduced motion.

## Out of scope

- Factory, dyehouse, or other narrow industry positioning.
- Fabricated customers, metrics, screenshots, certifications, or integrations.
- Purple/cyan effect stacks, particles, decorative carousels, or cinematic scroll.
- A second product design system separate from the existing dima tokens.

## Learned constraints

- Preserve the repository's current brand-token source of truth. Do not recolor the
  full product from a marketing-only task.
- The earlier orange request was conditional on this implementation having changed
  the brand to purple; git history shows that change predates this work.
- Generated imagery is editorial atmosphere, never product evidence.
