# Design decisions

## 2026-07-26 — Product-forward marketing

Adopt UpcyMan's level of visual storytelling through original Dima product visuals,
generated editorial imagery, Motion choreography, MagicUI-inspired primitives, and
shadcn behavior. Do not copy UpcyMan assets or brand language.

## 2026-07-26 — Truthful visuals

Generated raster images remain decorative. Product proof uses deterministic,
sanitized fixtures and existing product mechanisms.

## 2026-07-26 — Motion system

Use Motion's lazy `domAnimation` feature bundle and tween-based timing. No bounce,
magnetic cursor, cinematic scroll, or interaction-blocking stagger.
